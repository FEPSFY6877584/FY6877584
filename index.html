Conference paper:Formation of Galaxies

Abstract: Scientists have recognised galaxies for decades, but understanding how they were formed was another question entirely, based on components of the universe. I have researched multiple theories and studies on the formation of galaxies and have come to a solid theory on their formation from nebula and the mysterious Black Holes that cause all neighbouring stars and solar systems to orbit. This is all based on the Big Bang theory, where the universe is ever expanding from a central point which are the conditions scientists have speculated for the theory on the formation of Galaxies as the expansion of matter and dark matter leads to a cooling of the Universe thus allowing clusters of gas to form in space, eventually forming early-stage Galaxies.
