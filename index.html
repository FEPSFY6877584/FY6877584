How its Made: The Milky Way

Abstract: Scientists have recognised galaxies for decades, but understanding how they were formed was another question entirely, based on components of the universe. I have researched multiple theories and studies on the formation of galaxies and have come to a solid theory on their formation from nebula and the mysterious Black Holes that cause all neighbouring stars and solar systems to orbit. This is all based on the Big Bang theory, where the universe is ever expanding from a central point which are the conditions scientists have speculated for the theory on the formation of Galaxies as the expansion of matter and dark matter leads to a cooling of the Universe thus allowing clusters of gas to form in space, eventually forming early-stage Galaxies. Introduction: The formation of galaxies had puzzled for centuries as they weren’t even discovered until 1924 with the Hubble telescope (1) capturing the Andromeda Galaxy for the first time, which was groundbreaking and opened a rabbit hole of questions for physicists including how galaxies were formed. As of 2024 multiple studies on the formation of galaxies by respected organisations and scientists have come with interesting articles and publications that have led us closer to truly understanding our own existence and pushing the limits of astrophysics and even quantum sciences with the understanding of minute particles and energy of atomic matter forming the galaxies around a central point where matter is the most dense, in theory having a tremendous gravitational pull.
